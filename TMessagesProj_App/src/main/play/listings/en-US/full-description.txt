Mercurygram is an unofficial third-party Telegram client for Android. It is
open source, built from the official Telegram source code with the
de-googling patches of Telegram-FOSS forward-ported on top, plus a set of
privacy and convenience features of its own. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Telegram.

Notifications on this Google Play build arrive through Google's push service
by default, without any Google library inside the app: the app only asks Play
Services for a WebPush endpoint and a small gateway signs the pushes Telegram
cannot sign itself. Message contents stay end-to-end encrypted between
Telegram and the app. Any UnifiedPush distributor app installed on the device
can be picked instead, which keeps Google out of the path entirely.

Highlights:

• No Google Play Services dependency, no Firebase, no analytics, no crash
  reporting. The app talks to Telegram's servers and to nothing else unless
  you enable a feature that needs it.
• Message translation through privacy-respecting backends, including a fully
  offline translator.
• On-device voice message transcription, no cloud involved.
• Tracking parameters stripped from links before you open or send them.
• Local "Mercurygram folders" that never touch the Telegram folder limits.
• Search chats written in decorated Unicode fonts by typing plain letters.
• Extra options for chats, media, proxies and notifications, all documented
  at https://mercurygram.org/features/android/

Telegram Premium and Stars cannot be purchased through this app. Existing
subscriptions and features bought elsewhere keep working.
